2. Find genuine contradictions between claims (not just different framings of the same fact)
3. Resolve contradictions using source tier (Tier 1 beats Tier 2, etc.) and recency (newer beats older when tiers are equal)
4. Identify remaining gaps
5. Assess whether the evidence is sufficient to write a credible section
